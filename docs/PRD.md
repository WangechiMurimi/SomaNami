Project 2 Proposal
Project Name: SomaNami - An AI-powered learning app

Project Description:
People trying to learn something new, whether students working through coursework or self-learners developing a skill, 
often have access to the material they need but may struggle to understand, explore, or critically evaluate it. 
While existing tools support searching, summarizing, and questioning documents, these interactions may not adequately 
accommodate the different ways people develop understanding. This creates a need for more adaptable ways of communicating and 
representing information, enabling people to engage with the same material at varying levels of complexity and through different 
approaches without compromising its meaning.

User Stories:
1. As a user, I want to upload documents so that I can explore and understand their content without manually extracting or reorganizing information.
	Acceptance Criteria
Users can upload supported documents and receive confirmation of successful processing or clear feedback when processing fails.
Uploaded document content is retrievable with accurate references to its original source location.

2. As a user, I want to ask questions about uploaded documents in natural language so that I can clarify unfamiliar concepts and investigate specific information.
		Acceptance Criteria
Users can ask natural-language questions about uploaded documents and receive relevant, understandable responses grounded in the document content.
Responses include references to supporting source material, and the application clearly communicates when a question cannot be answered from the available documents.

4. As a user, I want to ask follow-up questions that retain the context of previous interactions so that I can explore topics progressively without repeating information.

Acceptance Criteria
Users can ask follow-up questions without repeating previously established information within the same conversation.
Responses remain relevant to the ongoing discussion and maintain contextual continuity across interactions.


5. As a user, I want to switch between different formats of explanation for the same concept, so that I can understand information through the representation that works best for me.

Acceptance Criteria
Context is preserved when switching views. When a user switches back to the original representation, then it reopens exactly where I left off.
Returning to a previous representation restores user's place of where they left off
